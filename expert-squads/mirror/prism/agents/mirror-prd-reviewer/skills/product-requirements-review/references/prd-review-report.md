# Independent Product Requirements review

Remain read-only over `.mirror/prd/*.md`. Review every assigned route document and its source packet.
Write only the review resource under `.mirror/prd/tmp/` and complete the typed Integrity protocol so the canonical result is persisted as a Catalog-readable Artifact.

The report contains scope and reviewed digests; verdict; Critical/Major/Minor counts; findings with
stable IDs, severity, exact document section, violated contract, evidence, user/implementation impact
and required correction; route/PRD/screenshot/asset identity checks; P1-P14 completeness; data,
interaction, state, navigation, accessibility, performance, copy/compliance and acceptance
implementability; the exact `greenfield_original` design contract; reference-observation versus
mandatory AInvest product/design-authority classification and authority revision; assumptions and
unsupported claims; and cross-route/shared-contract consistency.

Critical means the document cannot be implemented or accepted safely/correctly. Major means a
material ambiguity, missing behavior/evidence or inconsistent contract. Minor means a bounded quality
defect that does not invalidate implementation. Never edit the reviewed documents, soften severity
to obtain a pass, infer that a changed filename proves a finding was repaired, or accept external
visual parity as a Prism requirement.
