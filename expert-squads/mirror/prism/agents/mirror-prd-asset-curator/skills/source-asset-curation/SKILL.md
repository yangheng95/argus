# Source asset curation

- Read `references/asset-manifest-contract.md` before collection and use its identity, provenance, budget and output rules.
- Consume the mapper's cited AInvest product/design authority Artifact plus the accepted Task-wide reference-observation packet and its exact delivery-plan entry, assigned-surface, and member-route asset candidates. Do not reopen the source, search the web, traverse routes, or create a second discovery ledger.
- Classify logo, favicon, icon, image, Scalable Vector Graphics, background, and font assets without treating screenshots as reusable source assets.
- Record stable asset and screenshot IDs, route/region ownership, exact source URL or inline provenance, rights decision, local path, media type, byte length, digest, extraction method, and failure notes.
- Materialize every route-applicable AInvest asset explicitly approved by the authority and rights evidence. Reference observation alone never authorizes visual reuse or output branding.
- Keep assets under `.mirror/prd/assets/` and process reports under `.mirror/prd/tmp/`.

This contract distills `prism-team/asset-collection.md`.
