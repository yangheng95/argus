# AInvest feature mapping

## Selection and layer contract

- Read the accepted reference-route evidence and build a 10–15 item candidate pool from the projected AInvest knowledge Skill. Select exactly 2–4 contextually fitting High or Medium fit capabilities; record every Low-fit discard.
- Mark source product modules `[L1]`, transferable competitor capabilities `[L2]`, and selected AInvest capabilities `[L3]`. These layers govern product scope and behavior only; they do not assign external layout, styling, screenshot, or region-parity authority.
- Produce a complete module ledger with stable module/feature IDs, source, fit, and weight. The target balance is `L1=50% / L2=25% / L3=25%`, with at most ±10 percentage points per layer. Record and resolve every L2/L3 conflict.
- For every selected feature record wiki anchor, page mount point, trigger, mock dataset and samples, interaction/state flow, compliance hooks, fit rationale, module tag, and weight.

## AInvest hard rules

- Source every product name, plan tier, quota, Option Price Reporting Authority term, editorial fact, compliance document, token, and asset from `prism/shared/ainvest-wiki`; never reconstruct values from memory.
- User-facing spelling is `Aime`, pronouns are she/her, and `AIME` is reserved for engine/code tokens.
- Artificial Intelligence output must link `Disclosures-for-AI-Tools-on-AInvest-Fintech-Inc.pdf`; third-party broker redirects must link `Third-Party-Brokerage-Disclaimer.pdf`.
- Use `price-up` / `price-down` for stocks and `price-crypto-up` / `price-crypto-down` for crypto. Require the documented AInvest light/dark dual mode.
- Resolve the concrete light/dark values for every selected semantic token, the documented component variants/states, exact repository asset paths, market-color rules, interaction rules, and compliance surfaces. Each resolved item must include its Wiki source path, source digest, and authority revision.
- Write one structured cited product/design authority artifact with `authority_revision`, `source_files`, `semantic_tokens`, `component_contracts`, `asset_contracts`, `interaction_rules`, and `compliance_rules`. This is an immutable per-run projection of Wiki authority, not an independently editable token source.
- Planning, Product Requirements, Design, implementation, and every independent review consume that artifact. They must be able to render the selected routes as AInvest without Wiki access and without inventing a concrete value. Do not edit final Product Requirements Documents.
