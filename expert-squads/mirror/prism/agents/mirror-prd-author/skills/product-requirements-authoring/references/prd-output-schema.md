# Product Requirements Document output schema

Write the subsystem-closure-owned document set under `.mirror/prd/`; keep drafts and process reports in `tmp/`. A parameterized surface records its shared behavior once and carries a complete member-route evidence table rather than duplicating one document per content URL. The document set is accepted only as a connected whole; no member page is a standalone delivery unless the canonical closure evidence proves a one-route subsystem.
Use the following mandatory structure:

- **P1 Outcome and boundary:** end-to-end AInvest subsystem outcome, exact `subsystem_closure`, assigned surface and member route IDs, closure reasons, shared owners, reference/competitor summary, scope, non-goals and seven risk
  classes covering data, terms, scope, subjective acceptance, source drift, layer drift and conflicts.
- **P2 Information architecture and page family:** route identity, route pattern, page type, internal
  and external links, template relationship and system route-graph references.
- **P3 Ordered modules:** global header, sidebar when evidenced, ordered main regions, footer,
  overlays, and detailed module contracts with source/evidence tags.
- **P4 Original AInvest design authority:** `frontend_design_mode: "greenfield_original"`, exact mapper
  authority revision, AInvest identity, semantic tokens, components, assets, interactions, market
  colors, compliance and dual-mode rules, plus the explicit statement that reference observations
  provide no screenshot, region, geometry, styling, brand, or parity authority.
  Do not invent numeric tokens.
- **P5 Data and mock schema:** blocks, complete field table, types, nullability, units, formatting,
  sample rows, simulated-data labels, loading/empty/error rules and source anchors.
- **P6 Interactions:** page, control, table and complex-component behavior; trigger, precondition,
  state mutation, feedback, navigation, keyboard/focus, error/recovery and reduced-motion behavior.
- **P7 States:** default, hover, focus, active, selected, disabled, loading, empty, error, success and
  permission/access states where applicable.
- **P8 Device and acceptance boundary:** the exact authorized desktop viewport/device boundary. Do
  not add mobile/tablet delivery without explicit authorization.
- **P9 Performance and quality:** loading strategy, asset constraints, stability, accessibility and
  observable quality checks.
- **P10 Language and copy:** locale, terminology, dynamic values, disclaimers and prohibited claims.
- **P11 Acceptance:** exact closure-member equality; current Prism-authored render screenshots for every critical member/state; functional checks; canonical/deep-link entry; forward/back route transitions; shared data/state continuity; every material journey; recovery;
  keyboard, focus, accessibility, diagnostics and exact commands/evidence. External visual parity is
  not a Prism acceptance dimension.
- **P12 Assumptions and decision log:** every non-user decision, limitation, version/revision,
  source date, terminal review trace and unresolved risk.
- **P13 Research trace:** claim-level source references, asset `A###` manifest rows and screenshot
  `S###` ledger with route/state/trigger/path/digest.
- **P14 Source-layer feature map:** mandatory `[L1]`/`[L2]`/`[L3]` module ledger,
  competitor transfers, product-specific features, conflict decisions and weight audit.

Publish one durable Product Requirements Artifact that records route ID, document path/digest, source
screenshot, assets, shared contracts, journeys, acceptance evidence, and unresolved limitations. A
consumer discovers and exactly reads that Artifact from the Task catalog; the final message does not
repeat its machine-readable body. Missing required evidence is a blocker or explicit limitation, never
`N/A` used to hide an unperformed obligation.
