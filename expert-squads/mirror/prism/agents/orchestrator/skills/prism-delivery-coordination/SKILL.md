# Prism delivery coordination

- Bind `mirror-prism-ainvest`, the package's sole workflow, and immediately dispatch its reference-observation node.
- Treat the supplied page as the subsystem-discovery entry. Require one evidence-backed `subsystem_closure` containing every business-required associated subpage, shared capability, navigation/state dependency, and material end-to-end journey. A page is an internal work unit, never a separately accepted delivery.
- Prism owns original product design only. Clone, replica, port, screenshot-parity, reference-parity, and high-fidelity restoration belong to the dedicated Frontend Replica Expert Squad.
- Treat the terminal FrontendResearchBrief Artifact as the canonical system-project and subsystem-closure contract; never use its Turn-local authoring file downstream.
- Run Mirror Watch reference observation, general source-system research, and the mandatory AInvest feature/design mapper in declared order.
- Dispatch exactly one `mirror-prd-stage-planner` after source evidence is ready. Require one selected-source `prism/delivery-plan`.
- Require the plan to preserve exact closure membership, shared owners, cross-route dependencies, and joint journey acceptance; classify reference observations, competitor adopt/defer/reject inspiration, and the cited AInvest product/design authority separately; and declare `frontend_design_mode: "greenfield_original"`.
- The Planner is a Delegated Worker. Prism has no Requirements adapter, Architect adapter, RequirementSet, ContractGraph, Goal, Delivery Slice, or phase Task.
- Dispatch `mirror-design-page-designer` only with `mode: "greenfield_original"`. Target and competitor screenshots never become `design_source`, `visual_reference`, reference-region bindings, or formal comparison evidence.
- Dispatch every later Product Requirements Document, asset, design, implementation, integration, verification, and review node once for the whole subsystem Task after its manifest dependencies succeed. Require each stage to prove exact closure-member equality rather than representative-page coverage.
- A live repair uses the existing Agent-to-Agent coordination `continue` action in the same Session. An ended Turn reopens that same Session through `dispatch_agent` with `turn.kind=continuation`, `turn.authority.kind=prior_dispatch`, and the exact `turn.authority.continuation_dispatch_id`. Never allocate a replacement Session or rerun a successful predecessor.
- Derive route count and surface membership only from acquired evidence. Preserve every exact accepted identity and every associated subpage required by evidenced business dependencies; preserve shared contracts, state, navigation edges, journeys, limitations, and reference-product functional/content boundaries while converging the delivered visual identity to AInvest.
- Block only on false, contradictory, missing, corrupt, or inaccessible required evidence, failed persistence/tool execution, invalid owned paths, or execution that cannot continue.
