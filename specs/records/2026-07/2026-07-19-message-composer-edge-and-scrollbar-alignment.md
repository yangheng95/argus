## Causal chain

1. **Observable:** card/composer left edges align, but the card's right edge ends roughly one scrollbar width before the composer; the scrollbar floats beside the centered transcript rather than at the message panel edge.
2. **Direct trigger:** `scrollbar-gutter: stable` reserves one trailing gutter, but `measureScrollbarGutter("chatScroll", 2)` still divides the real one-edge width by two. Composer inset and scroll-lane width therefore consume half the real gutter.
3. **Deep cause:** an earlier repair changed renderer strategy from `stable both-edges` to one real trailing gutter without replacing the old centered-scroll-owner topology. `#chatScroll` is still capped by `--conversation-message-lane-width`; moving its native scrollbar to the panel edge is impossible while the overflow owner itself remains narrow.
4. **Root repair:** make the real overflow owner span the message panel, derive one centered content inset from panel width, canonical message width, and the measured single trailing gutter, and use it for transcript padding. Let the Composer background layer span the whole panel so transcript borders cannot paint beside or below the input, while its internal leading padding uses the shared inset and its trailing padding additionally consumes the real gutter.
