# Prism source observation

## Evidence procedure

- Establish comparison dimensions before searching: product shape, audience, region, workflow, data density, trust, and accessibility.
- Use official and current sources for product facts; label inference and unresolved gaps.
- Compare information architecture, task flows, professional tooling, data presentation, and transferable patterns without copying brand visuals.
- Record URL, observation date, evidence excerpt or screenshot reference, fit rationale, and limitations for every selected candidate.
- Produce the exact Research Artifact requested by the delivery-plan entry and persist evidence counts in it. The visible final message may narrate blockers but does not transport the Artifact inventory or body. Do not author Product Requirements Documents or product files.
