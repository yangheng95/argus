# Route design production

- Read `references/design-production-contract.md` before editing and execute both production passes and rendered verification.
- Execute only through Frontend Design mode `greenfield_original`; Prism never changes to `reference_parity`.
- Read the exact subsystem closure, complete Product Requirements Document set, classified product evidence, product/design authority, approved assets, shared owners, states, journeys, and critique.
- Declare one subsystem-level `DESIGN_INTENT` before editing and make an original information architecture, hierarchy, composition, density, shared shell/state/navigation, component, token, interaction, accessibility, and desktop-boundary decision.
- Use target observations for product structure, data, behavior, journeys, states, and accessibility. Use competitor material only for explicit adopt, defer, or reject decisions. Never use either as layout, geometry, styling, screenshot, region, branding, or protected-asset authority.
- Consume the mapper's cited AInvest product/design authority Artifact as every route's identity, token, component, asset, interaction, market-color, compliance, and dual-mode authority; do not load AInvest Wiki, invent concrete values, or create an independent brand.
- Produce every closure-member delivery-surface-owned, parameterized HTML entrypoint with local assets under the exact task-scoped `visual-html-skeleton` root supplied by the Frontend Design dispatch packet. Preserve every surface identity and member route's content/state evidence, shared-state continuity, deep-link/return behavior, and material journeys; capture every acceptance-required instance.
- Optional design-system context, brief detail, states/regions, screenshots not required by acceptance, or a pre-enumerated gap list may be absent. Record the limitation and continue without inventing content; stop only when required identity, evidence, owned paths, or rendering cannot be resolved.
- Launch the real design through `capture_frontend_visual_evidence` with `kind: "render_review"`, inspect its canonical task-scoped screenshots and diagnostics, exercise interactions, and correct every review finding.
- Do not claim completion while the Frontend Design fact status or canonical capture evidence is partial/inconsistent.
- Page files may be internal work units but are never separately accepted. Keep shared design contracts single-owned and prove the complete connected subsystem before reporting completion.

This contract distills `prism-team/designer.md`.
